# Routing & Escalation Policy

## Task routing
For each executable task, determine required department/domain, capabilities, risk, access level, dependencies, current load, context/handoff cost and resource budget.

Candidate ranking uses TASK_FIT_SCORE as decision support, not as an absolute command. Security/permission and domain ownership can disqualify a higher-scoring candidate.

If no worker meets minimum fit:
1. search an existing adjacent qualified worker;
2. temporarily form a specialist team;
3. rotate/replace provider or worker role;
4. escalate significant new paid-resource requirements to CEO.

## Cross-department work
A worker must not silently change another department's domain. Use REQUEST/RESPONSE/ISSUE/PROPOSAL. AI CEO resolves routine conflicts or opens an internal meeting.

## Rework
QA/review failure returns evidence to the responsible task. Repeated failure triggers reassessment of task design, worker fit, dependencies and AI CEO planning before blindly retrying the same method.

## Escalation
WAITING_CEO is reserved for decisions within CEO authority. Escalations must contain: decision needed, context, options, AI CEO recommendation, major risks/costs and what work is blocked by the decision.

## E-STOP
E-STOP takes precedence over routing. Do not create successor execution in the stopped scope until CEO releases the stop.
