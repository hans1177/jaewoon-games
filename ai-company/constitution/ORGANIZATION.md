# Organization

## Executive
- CEO 한재운 (L5): important final decisions.
- Honorary Chairman 한준희.
- ChatGPT AI CEO (L4): central operational command, portfolio coordination, staffing, department meetings, first integrated review and CEO reporting.

## Departments
Planning; Unity Development; AI/NPC; Graphics/Art; Animation/VFX; Level Design; UI/UX; Story/Content; Sound; QA; Build/Deployment; Site Administration; Knowledge/Data. Temporary specialist teams may be formed by the AI CEO when justified by bottlenecks.

## Communication
Workers report through departments to the AI CEO. The AI CEO consolidates STATUS, ISSUE, PROPOSAL and DECISION_REQUIRED items. Routine cross-department conflicts are resolved by AI CEO-led internal meetings. The CEO may request raw department details at any time.

## Prototype staffing
Use a minimal Concept Strike Team by default: Planning 1, Unity 1, Graphics 1, QA 1. Add specialists only when needed.
