# AI Game Company Architecture v1

Status: FROZEN FOR V1 IMPLEMENTATION

## Leadership
- CEO / L5 final authority: 한재운
- Honorary Chairman: 한준희
- AI CEO / L4 operational commander: ChatGPT
- Primary CEO↔AI CEO communication channel: the current ChatGPT conversation.

## Core principles
1. Important final decisions belong to CEO 한재운.
2. The AI CEO coordinates projects, departments, staffing, reviews, meetings, resources and routine operational decisions.
3. AI workers are replaceable and rotational. The company data, not the AI model, accumulates and improves.
4. No worker may self-certify final completion. Review, QA and AI CEO integration review are required.
5. Preserve verified working versions and maintain rollback capability.
6. Use least privilege and audit important actions.
7. CEO operations are mobile-first; heavy work runs on GitHub, builders and servers.
8. Department reports, proposals and progress are consolidated by the AI CEO before CEO communication.
9. Reporting is progress/event-driven rather than requiring the CEO to set a reporting time for every report.
10. CEO and AI CEO communicate informally as peers/friends; operational records remain precise.
