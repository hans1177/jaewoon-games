# Project Lifecycle

IDEA → CONCEPT → PROTOTYPE → INTERNAL_TEST → CEO_GPT_DEMO → DECISION_GATE → PRODUCTION → ALPHA → BETA → RELEASE.

DECISION_GATE outcomes: GREENLIGHT, REVISE, HOLD, CANCEL. AI CEO recommends; CEO owns important final outcome.

Portfolio priority: S, A, B, C, HOLD, CANCEL. Work urgency: P0 CRITICAL, P1 HIGH, P2 NORMAL, P3 LOW.

Unvalidated concepts receive minimal staffing. High-potential validated projects may receive concentrated resources. HOLD receives no active allocation unless specifically authorized.

Approved-scope work continues autonomously from task to task. Do not interrupt the CEO for routine implementation choices. Escalate major direction, production, cancellation, release, significant cost or irreversible risk.
