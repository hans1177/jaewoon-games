# AI Workforce Capability & Assignment System

## Principle
AI workers are assigned by demonstrated capability and task fit, not merely by an empty department seat. Worker identity/role is company-owned; provider/model is replaceable.

## Capability profile
Each active worker role maintains:
- technical capability scores by domain
- reasoning/debugging/review capability
- speed and context-handling characteristics
- known strengths and limitations
- preferred task categories
- unsuitable task categories
- company-observed performance evidence
- provider/model metadata, when useful

Capability estimates and company performance are separate. Provider/model changes may require capability reassessment; company project and knowledge history remains.

## Task Fit
Before assignment, the AI CEO evaluates:
1. task-required capabilities and risk
2. department/domain ownership
3. worker capability profile
4. verified company performance on similar tasks
5. current availability/workload
6. handoff/context cost
7. quality/security requirements
8. resource/cost efficiency

Result is a TASK_FIT_SCORE and assignment recommendation. A high generic capability score does not override poor domain fit or insufficient permission.

## Evidence-based performance
Track QA pass rate, regression rate, rework count, reporting accuracy, handoff quality, collaboration, difficulty-adjusted success, resource efficiency and relevant task history.

Do not permanently label a worker from a single result. Prefer repeated evidence and recency-weighted history.

## Staffing
The AI CEO may reassign, rotate, replace or temporarily add workers when fit, bottleneck, availability or quality evidence warrants it. Significant paid resource expansion remains subject to CEO cost authority.
